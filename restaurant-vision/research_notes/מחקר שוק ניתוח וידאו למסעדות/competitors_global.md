# Competitive Landscape: Camera / AI Vision Analytics for Restaurants & Hospitality (USA + Australia, incl. global vendors) — as of Nov 2026

> Method note for the report writer: WebFetch was blocked by the session network allowlist (vendor sites such as flowlinks.ai and restauranttechnologynews.com could not be opened), so the findings below come from web-search result summaries and the URLs they cite. Vendor claims are marked as such. Where aggregators conflict (CB Insights vs. Crunchbase-style sites), both figures are given. Israel-based vendors are covered by another researcher. Servo AI is HQ'd in London but has Israeli founders, so it is noted here.

## Q1. Which companies offer camera-based analytics for restaurant front-of-house (dining-room occupancy, table status, service speed, guest wait), and how close is each to our concept?

### Takeaway
No well-funded vendor owns "front-of-house table and service analytics for sit-down restaurants." The closest players are small, early-stage startups: Servo AI, Eyezilla, FlowLinks, DifiNative TableWatch, NymbleUp, HeadRandom and EasyVision. Above them sit two groups: (a) well-funded horizontal "AI on your existing cameras" platforms (Spot AI, Lumana, Solink, Wobot, Verkada) that offer generic occupancy and queue widgets, and (b) QSR and drive-thru speed vendors (Berry AI, Hellometer, HME, Presto). The one large-brand attempt at sit-down table and service vision, Presto Vision at Outback Steakhouse in 2019, was cancelled within about a week after a staff-surveillance backlash.

### Cited Findings

#### Tier 1: closest to our concept (FOH table and service state in dining rooms)
- **Presto Vision (Presto, US). Historical, the only big-brand FSR attempt.** Launched October 2019 with Outback Steakhouse as its first pilot. Cameras in the lobby and other areas tagged hosts, staff and customers in real time and analyzed their motion. Metrics included host availability, individual wait times, customer bounce rates and a forecast "guest experience score." Presto said data was stored only temporarily and auto-deleted after 30 days, and that there was no facial recognition — [BusinessWire launch PR (Oct 16 2019)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20191016005058/en/); [SecurityInfoWatch](https://www.securityinfowatch.com/video-surveillance/video-analytics/news/21111207/outback-steakhouse-pilots-video-analytics-solution-to-improve-guest-experience)
  - The system used existing cameras to track how long food takes to arrive and how quickly waitstaff tend tables — [CNBC (2019)](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/10/16/outback-steakhouse-is-testing-ai-to-help-improve-customer-service.html); [Customer Contact Week Digital](https://customercontactweekdigital.com/tools-technologies/news/outback-steakhouse-nixes-employee-surveillance-program-following-customer-backlash)
  - Outback cancelled the Portland test about a week after the announcement, following Facebook and Twitter backlash over privacy and employee stress. The spokesperson said "hospitality is best achieved by serving our customers' needs in the moment" — [Yahoo News (2019)](https://news.yahoo.com/outback-start-using-constant-surveillance-194200535.html); [CCW Digital](https://customercontactweekdigital.com/tools-technologies/news/outback-steakhouse-nixes-employee-surveillance-program-following-customer-backlash). Conflicting local report: [Willamette Week (Oct 2019)](https://www.wweek.com/restaurants/2019/10/21/outback-steakhouse-locations-in-oregon-are-being-used-to-test-new-employee-surveillance-technology/) quoted a manager saying it was being rolled out to five Oregon Outbacks. No pilot results were ever published.
- **Servo AI (London; founded 2020 by Amit Frenkel and Nizan Rosh).** Software that turns existing CCTV into real-time analytics. It combines footage with sales data to find service-journey pain points: delays in welcoming guests, slow food and drink delivery, payment bottlenecks. Targets retail and HoReCa on a recurring subscription. Raised €1.2M from Early Game Ventures (May 2025); totals are reported as €1.47M or $1.35M depending on source — [Romania Insider (May 2025)](https://www.romania-insider.com/business/early-game-investment-servo-may-2025); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/servo-ai); [Vestbee](https://vestbee.com/blog/articles/servo-secures-1-2-m)
- **Eyezilla (Loughborough, UK; founded 2018).** AI on existing CCTV and POS for restaurants and hospitality. Measures time between service steps against targets, follows customer journeys and staff interactions, flags training needs, and supports multi-site chains. About $200K raised (SFC Capital) — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/eyezilla/discuss); [G2 seller page](https://www.g2.com/sellers/eyezilla); [Welcome to the Jungle](https://app.welcometothejungle.com/companies/Eyezilla); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/eyezilla)
- **FlowLinks.** Reads existing CCTV feeds to show table status in real time, detects when guests leave and starts the turnover clock, flags uncleared tables, alerts floor staff and measures turnover time. HQ, pricing and customers could not be retrieved (site blocked) — [FlowLinks table-turnover page](https://www.flowlinks.ai/restaurants-cafes/table-turnover)
- **DifiNative TableWatch ("powered by SquirrelVision"), Intel edge partner.** Vision AI for automated table visibility and guest flow. Runs fully at the edge on Intel hardware without continuous cloud connectivity, and scales "from a single outlet to nationwide." A sponsored article (Nov 2025) claims 5–10% revenue uplift via faster table turnover. No pricing or customers are published — [Embedded Computing Design (sponsored, Nov 2025)](https://embeddedcomputing.com/technology/iot/edge-computing/transforming-restaurant-operations-with-ai-tablewatch-from-difinative-powered-by-intel-edge-processing); [Intel Builders solution brief](https://builders.intel.com/solutionslibrary/intelligent-automated-table-management-with-vision-al); [Intel edge AI catalog](https://builders.intel.com/ecosystem-engagement/solution-hub/edge-ai-catalog/partner-showcase/tablewatch-powered-by-squirrelvision-66)
- **HeadRandom.** A concept or product combining smart IP cameras, local edge AI and a cloud dashboard. Detects table availability and occupancy for hosts and reports peak hours, average table-occupancy duration and staff cleanup response times. Fuses POS transaction data with vision to confirm a paid table is actually cleared. Maturity is unclear; it reads like an early-stage or solutions-shop offering — [HeadRandom](https://headrandom.com/edge-ai-camera-restaurant-table-tracker)
- **NymbleUp.** Restaurant and QSR video analytics: real-time zone occupancy, dwell time, and seat occupancy with customer stay duration "to manage turnover and reduce waits," plus an alert system. No evidence that it detects waiter approach. HQ not confirmed (believed India-based, unverified) — [NymbleUp restaurant video analytics](https://www.nymbleup.com/restaurant-video-analytics-solutions/); [NymbleUp QSR](https://nymbleup.com/video-analytics-for-qsr)
- **EasyVision (San Jose, CA; founded 2024; about 6 employees).** "AI that connects to your existing security cameras and watches over your restaurant 24/7." Covers hygiene compliance and staff activity, with a free pilot on the customer's own cameras — [F4 Fund](https://f4.fund/startups/easyvision); [Premier Alts profile](https://www.premieralts.com/companies/easyvision)
- **Kibsi (Irvine, CA).** No-code CV platform on existing security cameras that markets "Table Management 2.0" for restaurants. Raised $9.3M pre-seed and seed (2023; NTTVC, Wipro Ventures, Ridge, etc.). Horizontal, not restaurant-only — [Kibsi restaurants page](https://www.kibsi.com/restaurants/maximize-restaurant-efficiency-increase-revenue-with-kibsi-computer-vision/); [BusinessWire (Jun 2023)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230622514376/en/Kibsi-Launches-AI-Platform-to-Help-Customers-Gain-Business-Insights-from-Cameras-After-Securing-9.3-Million-in-Funding); [OCBJ](https://www.ocbj.com/oc-homepage/9-3m-for-ai-computer-vision-co-kibsi/)
- **Custom-build precedent (SciForce, a dev shop).** Built a CV system for a restaurant chain of about 1,200 locations in 30+ countries. It detects guest seating, waiter approach, order placement and table cleanup, and switches table states ("Waiting," "Occupied," "needs cleaning"). It measures waiter response time (seating to first approach) and food delivery time, and alerts if no waiter approaches a new table within 3 minutes. This is nearly identical to our feature list, built as a one-off project — [SciForce case study](https://www.sciforce.solutions/case-studies/computer-vision-restaurant-service-optimization); [DEV Community write-up](https://dev.to/sciforce/optimizing-multi-zone-restaurant-service-with-computer-vision-for-hospitality-1ha8)
- **V-Soft Consulting (dev shop) for Tandoori Fusion.** Vendor case study claims a 50% reduction in wait times and a 20% drop in food delivery time — [V-Soft blog](https://blog.vsoftconsulting.com/blog/case-study-computer-vision-restaurant-analytics)

### Inferences

A per-table state machine (seated → ordered → eating → waiting for check → needs bussing) with waiter-needed alerts for full-service dining rooms is offered only by sub-$2M startups (Servo AI, Eyezilla), unfunded or undisclosed ones (FlowLinks, HeadRandom, DifiNative, NymbleUp), or custom dev shops (SciForce). That points to an unclaimed category, but possibly also a weak-demand or hard-to-sell category (see Q3).
- **Biggest near-term threats.** Horizontal platforms (Spot AI, Lumana, Solink, Verkada) already have occupancy and queue widgets, POS integrations, natural-language alerts, and restaurant sales motions. They could add "table zones" as a feature. Verkada's freeform AI alerts could approximate "table needs bussing" without dedicated models.
- **Partnership or substitute threat.** Toast and Ziosk already infer table state from POS and tabletop events, the "data the restaurant already trusts." A vision product should integrate with Toast (POS events plus vision ground truth) rather than compete with it. HeadRandom and Verkada already pitch POS × vision fusion.
- **Privacy architecture is converging.** Berry AI (on-prem server, no video stored, no FRT), Lumana (edge appliance) and DifiNative (fully edge) all match our planned design. This is now table stakes, not a differentiator. In Australia, the camera-AI conversation in licensed venues is dominated by facial recognition for gambling, so "no FRT, no stored video" positioning should help distinguish us there.
- **Australia.** No local FOH-vision competitor was found, so the main competition there would be global horizontal platforms (Verkada, Solink if active) and POS-based table management (Toast, Lightspeed, me&u and other local POS, not researched here).

## Q3. Which players failed, pivoted or were acquired, and what are the lessons?

### Takeaway
The clearest lesson is Presto Vision at Outback (2019). A sit-down FOH camera product framed around staff monitoring was killed by public backlash within about a week. Presto as a company later collapsed: Nasdaq delisting (2024), an SEC settlement over overstated AI claims (Jan 2025), and an asset sale. Other pattern-level lessons: occupancy and queue startups drifted away from restaurants toward stadiums, offices and CRE (Queues, Density, Butlr), where budgets are larger. Kitchen-CV players now fund themselves with non-dilutive debt (PreciTaste) or have gone quiet (Agot).

- **PreciTaste:** latest capital was revenue-based financing (Round2 Capital, Jul 2026), not an equity round — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/precitaste/funding-rounds/debt-2026-07). **Agot AI:** no news found after 2022 — [TechCrunch (2022)](https://techcrunch.com/2022/02/11/agot-ai-gives-restaurants-computer-vision-to-see-where-food-orders-go-wrong)
- **Winnow:** consolidating; reportedly acquired Lumitics (Jul 2026; single source) — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/c/Winnow)
- **Hellometer:** still very small after about 6 years (funding $250K–$1.13M) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/hellometer); [TheCompanyCheck](https://www.thecompanycheck.com/company/b/hellometer/ik6l7mzf2v1n16c6o)
- **Counter-example (what worked):** Berry AI's Culver's win pairs a clear revenue metric (speed and throughput tied to comps), on-prem processing with no stored video and no FRT, and franchisor-negotiated franchisee pricing — [Berry AI](https://www.berry-ai.com/culvers/); [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/culvers-berryai-vision-artificial-intelligence-rollout/817665/)

### Inferences
- **Lesson 1 (framing beats tech).** Outback killed a privacy-preserving (no FRT, 30-day deletion) system because the public story was "employee surveillance." Our product needs guest-centric framing ("no guest waits for a check"). Staff-facing value matters too, such as alerts that help servers earn tips rather than per-employee scorecards. Individual staff metrics should be aggregate-only by default. Restaurant staff and unions in the US, and Australian workplace-surveillance law (e.g., NSW Workplace Surveillance Act; not researched here), are likely stakeholders.
- **Lesson 2 (don't overclaim AI accuracy).** Presto's SEC settlement was over AI capability claims. Publish accuracy metrics honestly, and state any human-in-the-loop component if used.
- **Lesson 3 (budget gravity).** Several occupancy and queue startups migrated from restaurants to stadiums, offices and CRE, which suggests restaurant budgets per site are thin. Our pricing and unit economics (cheap edge box, existing cameras, minimal install labor) must work at roughly $100–200 per location per month.
- **Lesson 4 (QSR proved the model; FSR hasn't).** CV adoption in restaurants has scaled only where the metric maps directly to revenue (drive-thru seconds → comps). For FSR we need an equally crisp revenue story, e.g., minutes saved per table turn → extra covers on peak nights, or check-closing speed → turnover.

### Gaps
- No post-mortems found for small FOH-vision startups (none appear to have publicly shut down; most are simply quiet).
- No source explains why Agot went quiet, or whether its Yum! pilot scaled.
- Bite Ninja's current status (operating or not) is unknown.
- Whether Domino's ANZ Pizza Checker is still running is unknown. The iTnews headline "Domino's pizza AI prepped to go up in flames" appeared in results but its content was not retrievable — [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/dominos-pizza-ai-prepped-to-go-up-in-flames-539231).

## Q4. What do case studies and reviews say about measurable ROI?

### Takeaway
Nearly all ROI evidence is vendor-reported, and almost none is from sit-down dining rooms. Speed-of-service claims are the most quantified: Hellometer reports 47 seconds faster on average and "7 seconds ≈ 1% revenue"; Berry AI reports drive-thru time down 20–40% and comps up to 7%. Table-turn claims (+5–10% revenue, +10–15% turnover, −50% wait) come from sponsored articles or unsourced blogs. No independent or academic ROI study for FSR table analytics was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Hellometer:** operators improve service speed by 47 seconds on average, from a large sandwich-chain case where per-franchisee speed of service improved by "just shy of a minute." Its stated rule of thumb is that every 7 seconds faster yields about a 1% top-line revenue gain (vendor) — [Restaurant Dive PR](https://www.restaurantdive.com/press-release/20230126-hellometer-partners-with-intel-to-revolutionize-the-fast-food-experience-1); [BusinessRadioX interview](https://themag.businessradiox.com/news/making-the-fast-food-faster-with-hellometers-all-in-one-system-1651158548037.html)

### Inferences
- For FSR, the defensible ROI model has to be built bottom-up: minutes saved per table turn × peak-hour seat utilization × average check. Supporting levers are labor reallocation (fewer "floor-check" walks) and guest-experience metrics (time-to-greet, time-to-check). Expect buyers to demand a measured pilot (before/after on their own data), since there are no third-party FSR benchmarks to point to.
- The "7 seconds ≈ 1% revenue" style heuristic (Hellometer) is the kind of crisp claim that sold QSR CV. An FSR analogue would be "X minutes saved per turn = Y extra covers per Friday."

- No independent, peer-reviewed or third-party-audited ROI study of camera-based table analytics in sit-down restaurants was found.
- G2 and Capterra review content for Solink, Hellometer, DTiQ and Wobot could not be retrieved (fetch blocked); scores and complaint themes are unknown.
- No Australian ROI case studies for restaurant FOH vision were found.
- Industry adoption stats conflict: one 2026 summary cited the NRA (26% of operators use AI tools) against TouchBistro (87%), but primary sources were not retrieved, so these are omitted from the findings.
