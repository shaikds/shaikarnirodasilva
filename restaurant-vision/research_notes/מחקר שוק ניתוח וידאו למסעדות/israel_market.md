## 1. Size of the Israeli restaurant / food-service sector and recent trends (war, labor, wages, tipping)

### Takeaway
Israel has roughly 12,000–15,000 food-service businesses (all formats). Industry estimates put turnover at about ₪10–20B a year and employment at roughly 190–200K. The directly relevant segment is much smaller: Ontopo counts only about 2,000 full-service restaurants and cafés (venues with waiters). That is the realistic serviceable market for table-state and waiter-alert analytics. 2024–2025 were crisis years: bookings fell about 30% in H1 2025, about 4.6% of restaurants closed in 2025 and new openings dropped 41%. The sector also has a chronic labor shortage (8.9% vacancy rate, the lowest wages of any sector) and new upward wage pressure from the extended collective agreement for food service, 2025–2026.

### Cited Findings
**Number of businesses / segment size**
- About **12,130 active restaurant-sector businesses as of end of June 2023**, per the Small and Medium Business Agency, cited in a Knesset Research and Information Center (מרכז המחקר והמידע) report on the restaurant sector's manpower crisis (2023/24) — [Knesset MMM report](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/ad6da6f3-c7ff-ed11-815c-005056aa4246/2_ad6da6f3-c7ff-ed11-815c-005056aa4246_11_20240.pdf)
- About **13,000 businesses as of Q3 2021**. Until 2020, roughly 2,500–3,000 restaurants closed and 3,000–3,500 opened every year, so churn is very high (Dun & Bradstreet Israel, Oct 2021) — [D&B Israel](https://dbisrael.co.il/db-experts/editorial/restaurant_27102021/)
- An older figure of **~14.3K restaurants and cafés (vs. 12.3K in 2018)** surfaced in search summaries of ~2021 coverage. Exact original source unclear; treat as dated — [D&B Israel](https://dbisrael.co.il/db-experts/editorial/restaurant_27102021/)
- **Ontopo: about 2,000 "full-service" restaurants in Israel**, meaning restaurants and cafés that employ waiters and use a seated, waiter-served model (Ontopo figure reported in 2025 Israeli press coverage of Ontopo data) — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/shopping/article/ryahnx1111ex); [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2025_q2/Article-6ed947c929ac691027.htm)
- Other estimates: **~10,000 businesses with ~200K workers** — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/667985); **~40K waiters across ~15K restaurants** — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3706212). These conflict with each other and with the figures above.

Another source says turnover is "over ₪10B a year" — [Monopoli](https://monopoli.co.il/blog-business/how-to-start-business/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C). - Globes headline: "Tourists aren't coming, and the restaurant market is losing ₪3B a year" (date not visible, probably 2024) — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001384645)
- Event halls, a separate but adjacent segment: ~700 halls and event gardens, about 140–150K events a year, **~₪14B annual turnover and ~65K direct employees** (Event Hall Owners Association estimate, in a July 2026 article) — [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/55674); [BHOL](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/178075)

- **Jan–May 2025 (Ontopo data): occupancy down ~30% nationally. - Mako 2025 headline: "Drastic measures: 7 of 10 restaurants in Israel are fighting to survive" — [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2025_q2/Article-44870bfd507f591027.htm)
- **D&B: ~4.6% of Israeli restaurants closed in 2025, and 89% of those had operated 6+ years. New openings fell 41%** — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/financialconsumerism/news/article/20047301); [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-news/Article-3001e99a98e3c91026.htm)

- Northern restaurant owners were rebuilding cautiously as of ~Dec 2025, some reopening under new concepts — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/as-israels-small-businesses-struggle-signs-of-recovery-emerge/)

**Labor shortage**

- Early in the war, CBS recorded that **waiter/bartender job openings fell 28% and cook/chef openings fell 24%**. This is a 2023 figure, reflecting collapsed demand rather than easing — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-job-vacancies-slide-18-since-outbreak-of-war-with-hamas/)

**Minimum wage, tipping, service charge**
- National minimum wage **from 1 April 2026: ₪6,443.85/month (full-time), ₪35.40/hour** — [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9D)
- Extended collective agreement for food service: a **sector minimum hourly rate 10% above the national minimum from 1 April 2025, then 13% in the second stage and 16% from the third stage on** — [Kol Zchut waiters' rights](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D)
- Tips are counted toward the waiter's wage. The employer only tops up if tips plus wage fall below the minimum, so the waiter's income is mostly customer tips (the "tip law" regime) — [Kol Zchut on tips](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%A8_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D); [Mishpati](https://www.mishpati.co.il/article/16337)
- VAT on tips: in **May 2025 the Tax Authority decided not to collect VAT on waiters' tips** after negotiations with the Restaurants Association and the tax advisers' bureau — [TheMarker](https://www.themarker.com/career/2025-05-04/ty-article/.premium/00000196-99ff-d19a-abfe-9fffe3110000). Enforcement was postponed — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/sj9lqds3kl). A Knesset bill exempting tips from VAT passed a preliminary reading — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3706212)
- Restaurateurs argue that folding service into menu prices would sharply raise prices ("a hamburger would cost ₪150") — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/food/article/bjjokivggx)

**Regulatory context for cameras (privacy)**
- **Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 came into force on 14 August 2025.** It gives the Privacy Protection Authority (PPA) broad investigation and enforcement powers, including significant administrative fines. Several guides say it added no camera-specific rules but greatly raised enforcement risk — [O-N Law](https://o-n.law/privacy-protection-law-amendment-no-13/); [Companics](https://companics.co.il/amendment-13-privacy-law-security-cameras/); [Sec360](https://www.sec360.co.il/pages/%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%98%D7%97%D7%94-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9F-13)
- PPA **Guideline 5/17 (cameras in the workplace)** requires:
  - a written policy, notice to employees, proportionality, and a specific defined purpose
  - cameras only in work areas: not toilets or private offices, and rest areas only for an essential purpose
  - signage as the minimum form of notice
  - audio recording only within the Wiretapping Law
  - covert cameras generally prohibited
  - an employee right of access to footage of themselves
  
  Sources conflict on retention: one cites 14–30 days as reasonable, another says no explicit statutory period — [BAM Law](https://bam-law.co.il/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%99-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9F-13/); [LawReviews](https://www.lawreviews.co.il/article/cameras-workplace-privacy-law); [Cybertis](https://www.cybertis.co.il/cyber-insights/cctv-cameras-law-israel)

### Inferences
- **Realistic beachhead TAM is about 2,000 full-service venues**, plus a few hundred hotel restaurants and about 700 event halls. It is not the 12–15K total, most of which are counter-service, fast food or kiosks where table-state analytics matter little. At, say, ₪500–1,000/month this is a **₪12–24M ARR ceiling in Israel** if fully penetrated (illustrative arithmetic only). Israel suits a pilot and a reference market; the business case likely needs exports.
- The tipping regime matters for product design. Waiters are paid mainly by diners, so owners have historically treated floor labor as cheap and variable. The newer pain points are availability (8.9% vacancies) and the rising sector minimum (+10–16% above the national minimum). "Do more covers with fewer waiters" (alerts, bussing triggers, faster turns) fits this better than "cut payroll."
- **2025 occupancy collapse at chef restaurants (−53%)**: premium independents are financially stressed right now and are a weak early-revenue segment. Chains and groups with steadier traffic are the more likely early payers.
- **Privacy positioning**: "no faces, no stored video, edge processing" lowers Amendment-13 exposure. However, *staff response-time* metrics are still employee monitoring under Guideline 5/17. The restaurant will need a written camera/analytics policy, employee notice and a defined purpose. Shipping a ready-made Hebrew policy template and signage text would remove buyer friction.

### Gaps
- No official CBS (הלמ"ס) 2025/2026 count of restaurants/cafés or sector revenue was found. CBS publishes "accommodation and food services" aggregates, but no restaurant-only table surfaced. The Knesset MMM PDF could not be opened.
- No independent 2026 data on sector recovery (only Ontopo's self-reported 2025 index). 2026-specific closure/opening data not found.
- No data found on the share of venues that add an automatic service charge versus tips-only, or on adoption of pay-at-table (relevant to the "waiting for check" state).
- The exact phase-in dates for the 13% and 16% stages of the sector minimum were not confirmed.

## 2. Israeli companies doing computer vision / AI for restaurants, retail or hospitality (competitors and adjacent players)

### Takeaway
No Israeli startup was found that does front-of-house, camera-based table-state or occupancy analytics for restaurants. The niche appears **unoccupied locally**. Israeli CV strength sits in security video analytics (BriefCam, Agent Vi/Irisity, viisights), autonomous retail (Trigo, Shopic) and edge chips (Hailo, now being acquired by Microchip). Israeli restaurant AI startups target training (Bites), voice ordering (Hi Auto) and QR/ordering. Agot AI is not Israeli.

### Inferences
- **There is no direct Israeli competitor** in camera-based FOH table-state analytics, as far as Hebrew and English searches show. The nearest threats are:
  - generic VMS analytics (BriefCam/Irisity-style people counting and queue analytics) sold by integrators
  - "call-waiter" button or QR products (Platto) as cheap substitutes for the "needs waiter" alert
  - Tabit adding analytics on top of its POS data
- Hailo's sale to Microchip makes the long-term Israeli roadmap and pricing of Hailo modules (e.g., Hailo-8/8L on a Raspberry Pi-class edge box) slightly uncertain, but it likely improves supply-chain stability. This is worth noting in the edge-hardware decision.
- Bites' $40M raise shows that investors and chains (Shake Shack) will pay for frontline-ops tooling built by Israelis. Bites could also be a partner, since our data could identify where training is needed.
- Israeli security-analytics vendors' privacy and anonymization messaging (Irisity) is a credible precedent for our "anonymous by design" pitch.

## 3. Israeli restaurant software ecosystem and APIs (POS, reservations, delivery, scheduling)

### Takeaway
Two names dominate Israeli FOH software. **Tabit** (tablet POS) claims more than 45% of the Israeli POS market. **Ontopo** (reservations and waitlist) handled 8.6M reservations in 2025 and is effectively the national booking layer. **Beecomm** is the main legacy POS rival, alongside smaller players (Foody, Linet, Aviv, Mazmin, etc.). **Wolt** leads delivery by a wide margin, with **10bis** second. None of these offers a public, self-serve developer API. Tabit integration is **partner-gated**, so a formal partnership is the gating item for POS-linked features such as check status and turn-time revenue.

### Cited Findings
**Tabit (POS / restaurant OS)**
- Israeli company (registered 2012), founded by **Barry Shaked and Nadav Solomon**. Cloud plus tablets, "no central cash register", covering host stand to kitchen — [TheMarker 2019](https://www.themarker.com/technation/2019-05-01/ty-article-magazine/.premium/0000017f-f814-d47e-a37f-f93c7fb00000); [Obudget registry](https://next.obudget.org/i/org/company/514759661)

## 4. Who buys: chains, restaurant groups, hotels, event halls (personas and buying behavior)

### Takeaway
The buyer universe is concentrated:
- a handful of coffee and casual chains with 50–100 branches each (Aroma, Café Greg, Café Café, Landwer)
- a few multi-concept groups turning ₪300M+ a year (R2M, Kiso, Nono-Mimi)

Chains and groups are the most plausible paying early adopters, since they hold multi-site operations roles and the budget. Distressed chef independents are a hard sell in 2025–2026. No published data on tech budgets was found.

## 5. Israeli VMS/NVR integrators and security-camera installers as a sales channel

## 6. Price sensitivity: what Israeli restaurants pay monthly for POS / reservation / ordering SaaS (benchmarks)

### Takeaway
Israeli restaurant SaaS prices fall into three tiers:
- **Small add-on tools: ₪60–₪300/month** (digital menus, ordering systems, basic POS)
- **Mid-tier POS: ~₪200–₪900/month** (Foody ₪890 base, Linet ₪198 plus ₪110 per extra station)
- **Full Tabit stack: ~₪1,500–₪4,000+/month**, per a competitor's estimate

Ontopo reservations historically cost about **$200/month** (2022). Wolt takes 27–30% of delivery revenue. An analytics add-on priced at about ₪400–₪900 per venue per month would sit between a reservation tool and a full POS.

### Cited Findings
- **Tabit (Israel)**: **₪1,500–₪4,000+ per month software fee, plus hardware and setup** (estimate by competitor Mazmin, 2026, so treat with caution) — [Mazmin vs Tabit](https://mazmin.com/en/blog/mazmin-vs-tabit/). US listings show Tabit from $49–$69/month (US pricing, not Israel) — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10018657/Tabit/); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/retail-consumer-services-software/a/tabit/)
- **Foody POS**: **₪890/month base restaurant package (2 device licences, excl. VAT), +₪99/month per extra device** — [Foody](https://foody-pos.co.il/en/guides/restaurant-pos-israel)
- **Linet POS**: **₪198 for the first station** (software plus payment terminal), **₪110/month per additional station** — [Linet](https://www.linet.org.il/linet-pos/)
- POS comparison table (undated) lists starting prices of Kikar ₪60/month, TouchBistro ₪200/month and Lightspeed Restaurant ₪220/month. Hardware bundles run roughly ₪9,500–₪21,300 one-time — [Check-Box](https://check-box.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%95%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%A7%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%9C%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A4/)
- **Ontopo**: business model was a **monthly subscription of ~$200 per restaurant** (Calcalist, 2022); free for diners; deliberately unprofitable during growth (2022) — [Calcalist 2022](https://newmedia.calcalist.co.il/magazine-11-08-22/m01.html)
- Ordering systems: Shopiz **₪299/month with no commission** — [Shopiz](https://shopiz.co/). Another vendor advertises **₪140/month with 0% commission** — [FoodOrder](https://foodorder.co.il/); [WisePage](https://wisepage.wwcrm.co.il/academy/restaurant-ordering-system-2026)
- Digital menu screens **from ₪69/month** — [XVision](https://xvision.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%98-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA.html); a digital menu price guide — [Tafryt](https://tafryt.co.il/blog/digital-menu-price-israel/)
- Delivery commissions: **Wolt 27–30%** — [Globes English](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-uber-eats-gears-up-for-israel-launch-1001538202). The "no commission" positioning of ordering vendors shows restaurateurs' commission aversion — [WisePage](https://wisepage.wwcrm.co.il/academy/restaurant-ordering-system-2026)

### Inferences
- Israeli restaurateurs are **highly price-sensitive and anchor on flat monthly fees**. Vendors heavily market "0% commission" and "no setup". A **flat per-venue monthly fee** (e.g., ₪390–₪890 for a single venue, with hardware leased or bundled) will be easier to sell than per-camera or per-table pricing. Pricing above Tabit's low end (~₪1,500) is unlikely for independents.
- The edge box is a hardware cost the restaurant will see. Israeli POS buyers are used to one-time hardware purchases (₪9.5–21K bundles) *or* monthly leasing. Offering a ₪0-down hardware lease folded into the subscription matches local norms.
- ROI framing should be in shekels per shift: e.g., one extra table turn per Friday night, or one fewer waiter-hour at the sector minimum (~₪39–41/hour once the +10–16% sector premium is applied to ₪35.40). This is illustrative arithmetic, not a sourced figure.
- Chains will negotiate per-branch pricing similar to their POS deals. Expect discount pressure, and price the chain tier as volume per branch.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 price lists for Tabit, Beecomm, Ontopo or Tabit Reserve. Ontopo's $200/month figure dates from 2022.
- No data on what Israeli restaurants pay for security-camera maintenance contracts, a useful anchor for installer-channel pricing.
- No survey data on Israeli restaurateurs' willingness to pay for analytics or AI tools.
