## A. Section to paste

### AI analytics on our cameras

**What we do.** At [RESTAURANT NAME] ([ADDRESS / BRANCHES]) we use Restaurant Vision, a service
provided by [VENDOR LEGAL NAME], to analyse the live video of our existing cameras in the dining
room and at the entrance. The purpose is to run service better: to see which tables are
waiting for a waiter or need clearing, to alert our staff, to measure queue length and waiting
times at the door, and to count how many guests are seated.

**How it works.** A computer inside the restaurant receives the camera video and detects
people in each image as anonymous shapes. Short-lived random labels let it follow movement within
one camera for a few seconds or minutes, for example to tell that a party sat down at a table.
The video is processed in the computer's memory and discarded immediately. From it the system
produces anonymous events and numbers, such as "table 7: guests seated", "table 7: needs
clearing" or "3 people waiting, longest wait 6 minutes".

**What it does not do.**

- It does not recognise faces or identify anyone, and cannot tell who you are.
- It does not record or store video or images, and does not capture sound.
- It does not follow a person between cameras, between visits or between our branches, and
  creates no profile of you.
- It does not estimate age, gender, ethnicity or emotions.
- It is not used in restrooms, changing rooms or staff rooms.

**What leaves the restaurant.** Only the anonymous events and numbers described above are sent
to [VENDOR LEGAL NAME]'s cloud service (hosted in [REGION, e.g. EU / Israel / Australia /
United States]) so that our managers can see live status and reports. Video and images never
leave the restaurant through this system. The events do not contain images, faces, names or
any information that describes a person.

**Who has access.** Our managers and staff see table states, alerts and reports.
[VENDOR LEGAL NAME] processes the data on our behalf and only on our instructions, under a data
processing agreement. Nobody sells this data.

**How long we keep it.** Video: not kept at all (it exists only for a fraction of a second in
the computer's memory). Anonymous events: 90 days; reports and statistics derived from them:
400 days (about 13 months) [adjust if your agreement sets other periods]; then deleted
automatically.

**Security cameras.** Our security camera system ([CCTV RETENTION, e.g. "keeps recordings for
up to 30 days"]) is separate and is described in [LINK / SECTION]. Restaurant Vision does not
change what our cameras record.

**Questions and your rights.** Because the analytics do not identify anyone, we cannot link
anonymous events to you. For any question about this system or your privacy, contact
[CONTACT NAME / ROLE] at [EMAIL / PHONE / ADDRESS].
