# Customer signage: entrance sign and QR/link text

For: the restaurant (the controller). Hebrew version: `signage-he.md`.

Use this sign where Restaurant Vision analyses the restaurant's cameras. It does not replace any
security-camera (CCTV) sign that local law already requires; post it next to that sign.

**Rules for every version**

- Post it at every customer entrance, at eye level, readable before people enter, and near the
  dining-room cameras that the analytics use.
- Be specific. Say what the system does, not what it "may" do. In Australia the tribunal upheld
  the privacy regulator's finding against Bunnings because its signs said face recognition "may"
  be used and its privacy policy did not mention it.
- Name the restaurant as responsible, give a contact, and link to the website privacy notice
  (`privacy-notice-en.md`).
- Keep it true. If the setup changes (for example a new camera covers a new area), update
  the sign.

## D. New York City variant (voluntary)

**Why it is voluntary.** NYC Admin. Code § 22-1201 et seq. requires food and drink
establishments that collect "biometric identifier information" to post a sign near all customer
entrances. Images and video are exempt when they are not analysed by software that identifies,
or assists in identifying, individuals from physical characteristics, and are not shared, sold or
leased to third parties other than law enforcement. Restaurant Vision does not identify anyone and
shares no video, so the sign is not required. Posting this plain-language version is cheap
transparency. Do not use the city's "Biometric identifier information collected" wording: it
would tell guests the opposite of what happens.

The exemption is lost if the restaurant adds software that identifies people (for example a
face-recognition product on the same cameras) or shares camera footage with third parties. Ask
counsel before either.

> ### Notice to customers
>
> This establishment uses AI video analytics on its cameras to count customers and measure
> waiting and service times.
>
> **No biometric identifier information is collected, retained, converted, stored or shared at
> this location.** The software does not recognise faces and does not use face geometry, eye
> scans, voiceprints or any other identifying feature. It does not identify anyone.
>
> Video is processed in real time on a computer on the premises. It is not recorded or shared by
> this system. We do not sell, lease, trade or share any information derived from video.
>
> Questions: **[CONTACT EMAIL / PHONE]**. **[RESTAURANT LEGAL NAME]**.
