# Work log — 2026-07-08

## P10 closed — Martial arts & blast momentum (M8, developer-requested)

- **Request:** fight with arms/legs/heads; vanish to show speed; hold an
  attack to make it stronger (weak tap = no blast, long hold = blast); the
  blast sends the victim FLYING until they get up again, DBZ-style; more
  combos; and show the keyboard map at start. Spec'd first as M8 /
  FR-8.1–8.5 (spec.md §8.6).
- **Built:**
  - **Limb rig + pose system (FR-8.1):** the sword is gone — fighters get
    shoulder/hip-pivoted arm/leg groups with fist/foot tips. `syncMesh`
    poses the whole body per attack (`def.pose` in the attack table): jab,
    cross, roundhouse kick, haymaker, rising kick, headbutt; a walk cycle
    scissors the legs from real displacement, flight trails them behind.
    Heavy pressed mid-ki-dash converts momentum into a near-instant
    HEADBUTT (AC-8.1.2).
  - **Vanish (FR-8.2):** the body disappears through a dodge's early
    i-frames and afterimage ghosts (additive capsule fades) linger; ki
    dashes shed ghosts continuously.
  - **Hold-to-charge heavy (FR-8.3):** new `charge` fighter state; the
    controller starts it on heavy press and releases it with the key.
    Damage scales linearly tap ×1 → full (0.9 s) ×2.2; sub-10% charges
    quantize to 0 so uncharged heavies keep exact table damage (p2 stays
    frame-exact). Charge ≥50% turns a clean hit into a **BLAST**: the
    victim enters `flyaway` — launched at speed, tumbling, wall slams cost
    extra damage (skip-bounce off the ground when fast), then a `getUp`
    recovery that ends with brief rise i-frames. Getting hit cancels a
    charge (flinch/stagger overwrite the state, no stored release).
  - **Combo routes (FR-8.4):** `chainHeavy` in the attack table — pressing
    HEAVY during light2's recovery routes into an UPPERCUT (rising kick,
    launchVy 6.5 for smash→dash pursuit). Positional: light1 has no route.
  - **Key map (FR-8.5):** `ui/keymap.js` overlay greets every boot —
    movement/combat/Saiyan sections, the charge mechanic highlighted;
    dismissed by H/Enter/Esc/click, `H` reopens any time.
  - **FX:** blast boom + slam crunch (procedural WebAudio), gold blast
    burst, slam dust + camera shake, charge sparkles that densify and turn
    gold past the blast threshold.
- **Balance lesson (AC-8.3.4 amended):** the first rival implementation
  always charged its heavies (0.08–0.33 s minimum). Since a charge cancels
  on hit, every hold against a live opponent was free damage — the 20-duel
  S-3 band collapsed to 18/20 for the aggressive bot (resample 15/20, a
  real regression, not variance). Fix: the rival charges ONLY when the
  hold is safe (target stuck in a non-attack recovery) and throws the
  instant heavy otherwise. Spec amended first, then code; band verified
  ×3 after the retune.
- **Compatibility note:** player heavies now pass through a 1-tick charge
  state (tap release), so a buffered heavy comes out one tick (8.3 ms)
  later with identical damage — inside every existing test's step window.
