## Enhancement pass — visuals & feel (developer request: "too basic, hard to validate")
- **Why:** the developer found the blockout-only presentation (flat void,
  capsule fighters, corner-only HUD, zero hit feedback beyond a number and a
  material flash) made it hard to tell what was happening without opening
  the debug panel. This is a documented cross-cutting addition — new
  **FR-4.7 Presentation & feedback** in spec.md, explicit in its ACs that it
  must not move any FR-4.2–4.5 combat number.
- **Built:** `fx/textures.js` (procedural canvas textures — soft dot, blood
  blob, flame, checker floor, gradient sky — zero external assets, keeps
  NFR-1); `fx/vfx.js` (sprite particle pool for hit/block/parry/dodge
  bursts, blood spray with droplets that land as persistent ground decals,
  movement dust, special charge-up sparkle, a lightweight ambient dust
  system); `world/landmarks.js` (flickering brazier light + flame sprite at
  each named zone location — atmosphere and a navigation cue); Fighter
  visual upgrade (hilt+blade weapon replacing the plain box, shoulder pads,
  a team-color rim point light, shadow casting) — constructor/mesh only,
  no combat-state changes; `ui/hud.js` additions (lock-on reticle, in-world
  rival nameplate/health bar, damage-taken screen flash), all via the
  existing world→screen projection helper already used for damage numbers;
  `world/zone.js` textured/shadowed floor and walls; `main.js` wiring
  (resolver→VFX hook, movement-transition dust tracking, charge sparkle,
  shadow-casting sun sized to the arena).
- **Real bug found and fixed (not cosmetic):** enabling the shadow pass
  made headless software-rendered frames average ~180ms (119/120 frames
  exceeded the old 100ms clamp in a timing probe) — and `core/loop.js`'s
  fixed-timestep loop was **silently discarding** wall-clock time above its
  100ms/frame clamp rather than fully simulating it, meaning slow rendering
  was quietly slowing down gameplay itself, not just the frame rate. Fixed
  by raising the clamp to 300ms with a matching catch-up-step guard — this
  is a correctness fix for any slow-rendering situation (weak GPU, a busy
  background tab), not just this test environment, and was found *because*
  of the visual pass, not caused by wanting prettier graphics.
- **Regressions this exposed (test technique, not game bugs):** the larger
  clamp window meant single frames can now legitimately span 200-300ms of
  game time, which broke two wall-clock-timed sub-tests that assumed short,
  evenly-paced frames: `p1_movement`'s dodge distance/i-frame/cooldown check,
  and `p0_boot`'s hitstop-freezes-sim check. Both converted to deterministic
  stepping — p1's via the existing `step(n)` helper (same pattern as P2/P3),
  p0's by driving `loop._frame()` directly with synthetic timestamps (with
  `requestAnimationFrame` stubbed out) since hitstop/clamp logic lives
  inside `_frame` itself, not the generic step helper. Documented as a
  standing convention in plan.md's risk table: anything timing a window
  under ~300ms must not rely on real wall-clock waits.
- **Cosmetic fix during review:** shoulder pads were initially the same
  radius as the head (copy-paste), reading as extra heads in screenshots —
  reduced to a proportional size before shipping.
- **Verification:** p0–p4 regressions green across 3 full passes post-fix.
  Visually verified via fresh gameplay recording: checker floor gives real
  depth/distance cues, shadows ground the fighters, blood decals persist and
  scatter correctly across a fight, lock-on reticle and in-world rival
  nameplate/health bar track correctly, weapon/shoulder silhouette reads
  cleanly. Video + screenshots sent to the developer; single-file artifact
  rebuilt (esbuild bundle, smoke-tested headless, zero console errors) and
  redeployed to the same artifact URL.
