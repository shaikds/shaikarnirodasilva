# Work log — 2026-07-05

## P9 closed — Saiyan combat (M7, developer-requested expansion)
- **Request:** Sparking Zero-style mechanics — fly and fight in the air,
  rush the opponent, ki blasts, and Saiyan growth ("start from training and
  become super powerful as they fight"). Spec'd first as M7 / FR-7.1–7.4
  (spec.md §8.5). Original characters kept (the rival-prince dynamic, not
  the trademarked cast).
- **Built:**
  - Flight (FR-7.1): `F` toggles, `Space`/`C` rise/descend, flight speed >
    ground, ceiling clamp 14 m + zone-bounds clamp for flyers (walls are
    only 3 m). Melee gains a 1.8 m vertical-reach gate so altitude matters.
    Death cancels flight — the body falls. Flight is LOCKED until the
    genesis resolves: the first defeat awakens it (also keeps First Blood's
    arena sealed). White aura while flying.
  - Ki dash (FR-7.2): hold `Q` — full-3D rush toward the lock-on target at
    22 m/s, draining energy, stopping at melee range. The rival gets a
    `dragonDash` micro-GOAP action (cheap when it has energy).
  - Ki blasts (FR-7.3): `I` (hold to barrage) — 4 dmg / 8 energy / 0.18 s
    cooldown small projectiles through the SAME resolver rulebook (block
    70% reduction, dodge, parry). Rival gets `kiBarrage`.
  - Saiyan growth (FR-7.4): surge meter fills from damage dealt AND taken;
    at full it auto-TRANSFORMS — golden aura + light, ×1.25 damage, ×1.15
    speed, slow-mo announce moment. The rival transforms too, and pride
    burns: while the player is transformed and it is not, its meter races
    (+22/s). Zenkai: +60 power per defeat / +40 per victory, capped 1000,
    persisted in the rivalry doc, mapped to a gentle damage multiplier and
    a HUD power-level readout. Escalation resets per duel (transformations
    are earned in-fight).
  - Rival aerial AI: takes flight when the player does (or is 2.5 m+
    above), matches altitude, lands when the player lands.
- **Real bug found while testing:** the player's projectiles targeted the
  DUMMY whenever the rival's agent was disabled — now they aim at the
  lock-on target first (where you're looking), falling back to the live
  foe. Would have shipped wrong aim in free-roam sniping.
- **Test findings (both are the isolation conventions again):**
  1. Tests were setting `intent.rise/dash` directly — the controller owns
     intents and overwrites them from real input every tick; tests now hold
     `input.down.jump/dash` like a keyboard would.
  2. `revive()` keeps stale yaw; an attack consumed on the first tick after
     setup starts with that yaw and windup tracking (~0.14 rad) can't swing
     180°, so point-blank hits whiffed intermittently depending on what ran
     before. Real play never sees it (lock-on faces continuously while
     idle); tests now set yaw at setup like lock-on does. Diagnosed with
     three narrowing probes (solo melee → exact setup → full sequence).
- **Verification:** p9_saiyan 13/13 ×2 (flight lock/unlock/rise/clamp/land,
  aerial melee reach gate both ways, ki cost/cooldown/damage, 3D dash
  closing 22 m with drain, auto-transform + aura + ×1.25 damage, pride
  meter race, rival aerial pursuit, zenkai +60/+40 with persistence across
  reload). Full p0–p9 regression: ALL PASS.

# 2026-07-06 — Fun & minimal-beauty pass (goal-driven)

Developer goal: "minimal beautiful graphics but most important super fun,
interesting, evolving, and ready to play." Self-review found five drags on
the experience; all fixed, data/presentation only:

- **Transformations now happen in play, not just in tests** — surge gains
  retuned (dealt 0.5→0.8, taken 0.35→0.6/dmg): a contested ~130-damage duel
  reliably contains a transformation arc. (The staged capture on 07-05 had
  to seed the meter; real duels now get there on their own.)
- **The evolution is legible** — thin gold surge meter under the energy bar
  (glows at full), rival nameplate shows its level (`SERAK · LV3`), player
  power level already on HUD.
- **Never lose your nemesis** — red edge chevron points toward the rival
  whenever it's off-screen or >12 m away (handles behind-camera). Fun over
  realism: it's a rivalry, you sense each other.
- **Tempo** — ground speed 6→7 m/s (free-roam was dead time); ki dashes now
  leave a sparkle motion trail (readability of the rush).
- **Minimal-beauty grade** — ACES filmic tone mapping (exposure 1.15) and a
  single blood-moon sprite low on the horizon: two changes, most of the
  mood. (Top-level `await import` for the moon texture was rejected —
  esbuild IIFE bundles can't take top-level await; static import instead.)
- **Ready to play** — the awakening now shows a prominent 8 s prompt with
  all four new controls (F / Space·C / Q / I) instead of one subtitle line;
  prompts clear on encounter start so taunts own the screen.
- **Verification:** full p0–p9 regression ALL PASS; staged screenshot
  confirms chevron, surge meter, moon, grade, and the awakening prompt in
  one frame; artifact rebuilt and redeployed.
