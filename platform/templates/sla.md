## 4. Change Management

Changes to scope after contract signing will be handled as follows:

1. Client submits a **Change Request** (in writing - email or Slack)
2. SilvA.i provides an **Impact Assessment** within 2 business days:
   - Additional hours/cost
   - Timeline impact
   - Any risks
3. Client **approves or declines** the change
4. Approved changes are documented and added to this agreement

**Small changes** (< 2 hours effort): Included in project scope at no additional cost.
**Medium changes** (2-8 hours): Quoted and billed separately.
**Large changes** (8+ hours): Require a separate mini-proposal.
